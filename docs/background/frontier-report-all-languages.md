# Skeptical checks redraw four endangered-language research maps

Of the 17 morphology paper angles first proposed for Kamas, Selkup, Texistepec Popoluca and Dolgan, **only five survived independent falsification as OPEN-VERIFIED**, and one of those survived only after its premise was rewritten. Nine ended as PARTIAL, most of them downgraded because a second pass found prior or in-progress work. Three remain UNVERIFIED because the decisive source could not be reached. The best remaining projects for two Berkeley linguists working from home on open data are these. First, **a corpus study of Dolgan evidentiality set against INEL Evenki**, which is the cleanest verified gap and has about 98k glossed Dolgan tokens and 93k Evenki tokens with matching tiers. Second, **a productivity and dialectometry study of Selkup Aktionsart suffixes on INEL's 81k words**, for which every predecessor found is qualitative. Third, **an apparent-time test of case and object-marking change in Dolgan**, which needs careful framing around Karina Sheifer's unpublished DOM work. Kamas and Texistepec make weaker standalone bets. Kamas has a pre-shift layer of only about 2,500 words in INEL. Texistepec's open corpus is about 1.1 hours from a single fieldwork collection. Both still offer one defensible angle each: post-shift causative productivity for Kamas, and a corpus test of Reilly's morphophonologically conditioned ergative split for Texistepec. A recurring lesson: the most dangerous prior art for these languages now sits in cross-linguistic DoReCo and NLP papers that list the language only in a sample table. Searches by language name miss them. "OPEN-VERIFIED" in this report means that no falsifier turned up in the searches that were run. It is not proof of absence.

Two came from **conference talks and work-in-progress lists**: Riaposov and Sheifer. The practical consequence for the Berkeley pair is that any novelty claim must be checked against all three kinds of source, not just against journal articles that name the language in the title.

## Kamas: a pre-shift literature meets a post-shift corpus

### What has been researched

Modern Kamas grammar is almost entirely the work of **Gerson Klumpp**. His dissertation on converb–auxiliary aspect constructions (2002, 352 pp.) is based mostly on Donner's 1912–14 data ([review](https://users.utu.fi/jumyli/wp-content/uploads/sites/1378/2022/09/einedeta.pdf)). He has also written on grammaticalized aspect markers ([Klumpp 2005](https://real.mtak.hu/54908/)), valency operators ([Klumpp 2016](https://pressto.amu.edu.pl/index.php/linpo/article/download/linpo-2016-0014/28540/70845)), demonstratives ([Klumpp 2015](https://ojs.utlib.ee/index.php/jeful/article/view/15191)), a mirative connector ([Klumpp 2024](https://ebook.bibl.u-szeged.hu/index.php/btk-magyarnyelviirodalmi-intezet/catalog/book/314/chapter/568)) and the Oxford Guide chapter ([OUP](https://academic.oup.com/book/43672/chapter/366315545)). His full LMU list adds pieces the first pass missed: on the possessive accusative (1999), old negation and new tenses (2001), valency derivation (2003), a list of tense-aspect forms (2005), and the instrument noun *-(p)sVn* (2009) ([LMU list](https://www.finnougristik.uni-muenchen.de/personen/ehemalige/gerson_klumpp/publikationen/index.html)). **Ago Künnap** supplied the 1960s fieldwork with the last speaker, Klavdiya Plotnikova, the text editions, a 1977 survey of mood and tense markers, which already notes that the conjunctive was replaced by indicative + Russian бы ([kirj.ee](https://kirj.ee/wp-content/plugins/kirj/pub/so.fen.ug-1977-2-124-134_20240223170426.pdf)), and a 1999 grammar sketch ([Glottolog ref](https://glottolog.org/resource/reference/id/35966)). Dregval' & Matveev 1967 had already reported the plural *-zaŋ* giving way to *-i* ([kirj.ee](https://kirj.ee/wp-content/plugins/kirj/pub/so.fen.ug-1967-1-41-43_20240208161903.pdf)), so that finding cannot be presented as new.

The Hamburg INEL era added the corpus and a handful of corpus papers. These cover number marking on Russian borrowings ([Arkhipov 2023](https://www.degruyterbrill.com/document/doi/10.1515/9783110986600-003/html)), existential, locative and possessive predication contrasting "Full" and "Reactivated" Kamas ([Däbritz & Wagner-Nagy 2024](https://benjamins.com/catalog/jul.00024.dab)), information status ([Wegener 2017](https://www.slm.uni-hamburg.de/en/inel/aktivitaeten/publikationen.html)), clause linkage, stress ([Normanskaya 2022](https://ojs.bibl.u-szeged.hu/index.php/stualtaica/article/view/44331)) and a run of Arkhipov talks on stress through 2025. **INEL Kamas 2.0** contains about 49,000 Kamas words, of which **about 46,500 are post-shift and only about 2,500 pre-shift**, with Leipzig-style mb/mp/ge tiers, under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 ([INEL documentation](https://s3-uhh.lzs.uni-hamburg.de/gwiss-inel-corpora/remote/kamas-2.0/documentation/kamas-2.0-documentation.pdf)). A DoReCo time-aligned subset is also available ([DoReCo](https://doreco.huma-num.fr/languages/kama1351)). That asymmetry is the single most important constraint on any Kamas project. Klumpp's work deliberately uses pre-shift data, and INEL-era work mostly uses Plotnikova. The only studies that set the two layers against each other are Klooster's qualitative MA thesis on about seven minutes of audio ([Klooster 2015](https://dspace.ut.ee/bitstream/10062/47767/1/tiina_klooster._2015.pdf)), Klumpp's REMODUS teaching handout ([REMODUS](https://remodus.univie.ac.at/fileadmin/user_upload/p_remodus/5.2_Klumpp_LangAttr_Kamas.pdf)), and Däbritz & Wagner-Nagy for a single construction family.

### Explicit calls for further research, and their fate

**Klooster 2015** called for "future more in-depth quantitative research" on Plotnikova's recordings. This is **PARTIAL**: later quantitative work covers only borrowed-noun number and predication. Klooster also asked whether Plotnikova uses aspect markers in a Russian-like way. The first pass treated that as open. The verification pass found that **Klumpp's own dissertation, chapter VII (pp. 327 and 334), already says no converb constructions remain in her speech and that her usage appears governed by Russian aspect** ([review](https://users.utu.fi/jumyli/wp-content/uploads/sites/1378/2022/09/einedeta.pdf)). Klooster's page reference points into that chapter, so the qualitative claim has been made. Klumpp's REMODUS handout asks for "a closer inspection of changes from pre- to post-shift Kamas". This remains PARTIAL. **Klumpp 2016** asked for an areal comparison of valency operators and explicitly excluded post-shift data. The areal comparison remains open. The post-shift side is now **partly under study by Aleksandr Riaposov, whose 2024 and 2026 talks address reflexive strategies in pre- vs post-shift Kamas** ([INEL talks](https://www.slm.uni-hamburg.de/inel/aktivitaeten/konferenzbeitraege.html)). Klooster's point that attrition over time cannot be assessed is a structural limitation, not a gap: all post-shift recordings fall within 1964–1970.

### Kamas against Tundra Nenets

Nikolaeva's *Grammar of Tundra Nenets* covers three cross-reference series (subject, object, reflexive), 15 non-indicative mood/evidential forms and valency derivation ([LINGUIST review](https://listserv.linguistlist.org/pipermail/linguist/2015-November/077187.html)). Northern Samoyedic object conjugation also has its own diachronic literature ([Wratil, LangSci](https://langsci-press.org/catalog/view/173/1279/1053-1)). On paper, the sharpest divergence is object agreement. Verification showed, however, that **the divergence partly reflects the language rather than neglect**. Katzschmann, citing Künnap 1978, reports that Kamas conjugates transitive verbs in the subjective conjugation and shows objective forms only exceptionally ([core](https://core.ac.uk/download/147052979.pdf)). Residues do exist: negative-auxiliary paradigms in both conjugations in Künnap 1966 ([dspace](https://dspace.ut.ee/bitstreams/42950185-c730-4300-961b-3f66bf908d80/download)), and an SBJ2SG.OBJ form glossed from Joki 1944 by Klumpp 2012 ([JEFUL](https://ojs.utlib.ee/index.php/jeful/article/download/jeful.2012.3.1.17/10329/15236)). The dual appears in Donner's paradigms but rarely in his texts ([REMODUS](https://remodus.univie.ac.at/fileadmin/user_upload/p_remodus/5.2_Klumpp_LangAttr_Kamas.pdf)). Usenkova's Samoyedic evidentiality survey mentions Kamas only as undocumented ([core](https://core.ac.uk/download/42946818.pdf)). The Nenets side of this comparison is thinly verified, because Salminen's inflection work and Nenets modality studies were not searched.

## Selkup: the best-mapped language still lacks verbal-derivation statistics

### What has been researched

Selkup has the densest and most quantitative literature of the four. Reference grammars run from Kuznecova, Helimski & Grushkina 1980 to Klumpp & Budzisch 2023 ([INEL Selkup documentation](https://s3-uhh.lzs.uni-hamburg.de/gwiss-inel-corpora/remote/selkup-2.0/documentation/INEL_Selkup_Corpus.pdf); [Budzisch page](https://www.slm.uni-hamburg.de/ifuu/personen/josefina-budzisch.html)). The Hamburg group has produced corpus studies of attributive possession ([Budzisch 2021 FUF](https://journal.fi/fuf/article/view/97373)), definiteness ([Budzisch 2021](https://ebook.ek.szte.hu/index.php/btk-magyarnyelviirodalmi-intezet/catalog/book/129)), DOM on 179 objects ([Wegener 2018](https://ojs.utlib.ee/index.php/jeful/article/download/jeful.2018.9.1.07/10181/14949)), converbs ([Behnke 2020](https://ojs.utlib.ee/index.php/jeful/article/download/jeful.2020.11.2.06/11954/18407)), clause linkage ([GEPRIS](https://gepris.dfg.de/gepris/projekt/445353352/ergebnisse)) and the lV-form. The Novosibirsk, Tomsk and Moscow side has contributed Lingvodoc-based number studies ([Kovylin 2025a](https://ispranproceedings.elpub.ru/jour/article/download/1946/1795); [2025b](https://ispranproceedings.elpub.ru/jour/article/download/2010/1874)), the desiderative ([Kovylin 2024](https://philology.nsc.ru/journals/spj_en/article.php?id=2193)), evidential pleonasm ([Ilyina 2019](https://fehu.philology.nsc.ru/journals/spj/pdf/2019_2/18.pdf)), quotatives and borrowed particles ([Urmanchieva 2021](https://alp.iling.spb.ru/articles/xvii2/09.en.html); [2025](https://trudy.ruslang.ru/en/node/2122)) and a diachronic reconstruction of conjugation ([Urmanchieva 2023](https://alp.iling.spb.ru/static/alp_XIX_1/08.pdf)). A 2026 Cambridge Scholars volume by Wagner-Nagy and Budzisch compares six Samoyedic languages; it has no conjugation chapter ([sample](https://cspcontents.s3.eu-west-1.amazonaws.com/master/samples/978-1-0364-6600-8-sample.pdf)). **INEL Selkup 2.0 contains 352 glossed transcripts, 89 speakers and 81,498 words**, with Southern texts dominant (242 of 352) and one speaker contributing 100 texts ([documentation](https://s3-uhh.lzs.uni-hamburg.de/gwiss-inel-corpora/remote/selkup-2.0/documentation/INEL_Selkup_Corpus.pdf)). The separate Selkup Language Corpus adds 154 published texts ([openpolar](https://openpolar.no/Record/ftunihamburgdata:oai:fdr.uni-hamburg.de:14222)). Selkup was also in the LowResourceEval-2019 shared task, so a pure NLP segmentation paper would not be novel ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.11285v1)).

### Explicit calls for further research, and their fate

The first pass found 12 explicit future-work statements. Several have since been partly met. Behnke's call for a better classification of finite verbs in converbal constructions was followed by Behnke & Budzisch 2023–2024 (**PARTIAL**). Karpinec & Manokhina's note that Ivankino morphology had been left out was taken up by Kovylin's isogloss work ([Kovylin 2026](https://ispras.ru/proceedings/docs/2026/38/4-2/isp_38_2026_4_267.pdf)) (**PARTIAL**). Wegener's slides asked which factors govern subjective vs objective conjugation ([slides](https://sisu.ut.ee/wp-content/uploads/sites/264/wegener_conjugation_in_selkup.pdf)). The first pass rated this OPEN with moderate-high confidence. **The verification pass downgraded it to PARTIAL: Budzisch 2021 has a dedicated §6.3.2 "Konjugation", with tables of conjugation by object status and definiteness, on a 77k-token N/C/S corpus.** É. Kiss 2013 had also already analysed Selkup object agreement as secondary-topic marking, following Dalrymple & Nikolaeva. Usenkova's call for work on auditive combinatorics ([core](https://core.ac.uk/download/42946818.pdf)) is PARTIAL. An ISTINA presentation on Northern Selkup tense, mood and evidentiality by period, 1925–2015, is the closest competitor; its authors are unidentified. Bolsunovskaya's admission that "characterizational" Aktionsart verbs were not analysed ([conclusion](https://portal.tpu.ru/SHARED/b/BOLSUNOVSKL/research/Tab1/7%D0%97%D0%B0%D0%BA%D0%BB%D1%8E%D1%87%D0%B5%D0%BD%D0%B8%D0%B5.pdf)) remains **OPEN**. Kovylin's call to specify the kinship plurals *-sat/-mɨt* in Narym is also OPEN, but it fits INEL poorly because INEL has only 10 Central speakers. The DFG project report's call to clarify dialectal converb variation is likewise OPEN, and the figures conflict: Behnke's SLC data put the South far ahead, while INEL gives North ≈ South > Central. Diachronic puzzles such as the 1Sg *-ŋ* and the auditive's origin are open but not corpus-tractable.

### Selkup against Tundra Nenets

The Nenets link between object agreement and information structure is the centrepiece of Dalrymple & Nikolaeva's *Objects and Information Structure* ([catalog](https://www.ibs.it/objects-information-structure-libro-inglese-mary-dalrymple-irina-nikolaeva/e/9780521199858)). For Selkup, it is now covered by a descriptive cross-tabulation (Budzisch) and a theoretical reanalysis (É. Kiss) but not by a multifactorial model. A Forest Nenets corpus study of object agreement and referential devices ([Toldova 2026](https://vestnik.philol.msu.ru/issues/VMU_9_Philol__2026_01_3.pdf)) offers a direct methodological template. Non-verbal predication, clause linkage, negation and reflexives are all covered for Selkup. Valency alternations, clitics and Russian-loan morphology are the thin rows, but the valency row was never searched directly, so it is UNVERIFIED rather than open. **Selkup's map diverges least from its Nenets comparison**, and the remaining gaps lie in verbal derivation and contact morphology rather than in the core inflectional categories.

## Texistepec: one fieldworker's corpus, now mined by typologists

### What has been researched

The analytic literature traces almost entirely to **Søren Wichmann's 1990s fieldwork**. Its outputs are a text collection (1995/1996), an analytic dictionary (2002), the ALI-Colmex sketch with a 594-sentence questionnaire (2007), a phonology paper on stress (1994), two papers on the grammaticalization of an auxiliary paradigm ([Wichmann 2003](https://pure.mpg.de/pubman/item/item_1555814_2)), a speaking-vs-writing paper (2000), and a glossed IJAL text with Boudreault (2017) ([Wichmann publications](https://soerenwichmann.com/publications/)). The other analytic contribution is **Ehren Reilly's** set of OT papers arguing that the alternation between the two agreement paradigms is conditioned morphophonologically rather than by syntactic Case. These are BLS 2004 ([ROA](https://roa.rutgers.edu/article/view/676.html)) and *Lingua* 2007, whose abstract frames the evidence cross-linguistically and mentions no corpus counts ([ISC](https://search.isc.ac/inventory/10/1290587.htm)). No full reference grammar exists. The **DoReCo dataset** (CC BY 4.0) is the main open corpus ([DoReCo](https://doreco.huma-num.fr/languages/texi1237)). Wav2Gloss used 0.84 h for training and 0.26 h for dev+test, which implies roughly 1.1 hours ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2403.13169)). The exact token count was not visible and must be checked on download. A second resource is the **~900-word canonical segmentation set of Mager et al. 2020, which is Texistepec elicitation from ALI 2007**. Their mention of "Pisaflores, Veracruz" refers to the Tepehua dataset ([ar5iv](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2010.02804)). The data release names no explicit license ([canseg](https://turing.iimas.unam.mx/wix/canseg)). Wichmann's own list shows no Texistepec-specific work after the 2022 DoReCo release. The language's recent bibliography is therefore made up of cross-linguistic studies: Wav2Gloss, Liu & Dorr 2024, Paschen 2025 and Becker's talks.

### Explicit calls for further research, and their fate

This is the weakest part of the evidence. **Neither pass could read the full texts of Reilly 2004/2007, Wichmann 2003/2000 or the DUMAS mémoire "Les relatives du popoluca de Texistepec"**, so no verbatim future-work statements from Texistepec-specific papers are verified. The checkable items are as follows. Mager et al. framed low-resource segmentation as an open challenge. **This is now PARTIAL, because Liu & Dorr 2024 report per-language Popoluca results: CRF F1 0.88 on random splits, falling to 0.80 on adversarial splits, and Transformer 0.50 falling to 0.36** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2404.09371)). Reilly's typological prediction implies a corpus test that nobody has run (OPEN-VERIFIED). Wichmann 2000's observation that subordination, passives and participles are rare in speech is PARTIAL or UNVERIFIED, pending the DUMAS mémoire, whose author and year are unknown behind a bot wall ([DUMAS](https://dumas.ccsd.cnrs.fr/dumas-03219910)). The call for further documentation is open, and INALI's 2024 community planning council mentions no new corpus ([INALI](https://www.inali.gob.mx/detalle/se-conformo-el-consejo-de-planificacion-linguistica-comunitaria-de-la-lengua-waa-oot-popoluca-de-texistepec)).

### Texistepec against Sierra Popoluca and other Mixe-Zoquean

**This is the widest descriptive gap of the four comparisons.** Boudreault's 719-page *Grammar of Sierra Popoluca* has chapters on verbal derivation, valency increase, the verbal template and affix ordering, noun incorporation, verb serialization, dependent verbs, and topic and focus ([De Gruyter](https://www.degruyterbrill.com/document/doi/10.1515/9783110766271/html)). Chiapas Zoque ([Faarlund 2012](https://glottolog.org/resource/reference/id/313123)) and Ayutla Mixe ([Romero-Méndez](https://arts-sciences.buffalo.edu/content/dam/arts-sciences/linguistics/AlumniDissertations/Romero-Mendez%20dissertation.pdf)) have grammars too. For Texistepec, no dedicated study was found on applicatives, causatives, incorporation, serialization, the template, nominal morphology, topic/focus or contact morphology. But first description does exist, since ALI 2007 glosses applicative, double applicative and causative morphemes ([ALI excerpt](https://arqueologiamexicana.mx/sites/default/files/banco_imagenes/popoluca-de-texistepec.pdf)). The likeliest falsifier, Zavala & Wichmann's "A typological overview of Mixe-Zoquean languages" (pp. 627–686), was paywalled. The comparative angle therefore stays **UNVERIFIED**. A local note for Berkeley readers: Gutiérrez-Morales's 2008 Berkeley dissertation covers Nahuatl and Spanish contact in *Sierra* Popoluca, not Texistepec ([AfBo](https://afbo.info/sources/gutierrezmoralesborrowing2008)).

## Dolgan: a corpus grammar exists, but evidentiality and change remain uncounted

### What has been researched

Dolgan has a modern, corpus-based reference grammar ([Däbritz 2022](https://openpolar.no/Record/ftzenodo:oai:zenodo.org:8232753)) and a 2023 sketch ([Däbritz 2023](https://dergipark.org.tr/tr/download/article-file/3631398)). There is a contact dissertation ([Stapert 2013](https://universiteitleiden.nl/en/research/research-output/humanities/contact-induced-change-in-dolgan)), and a run of single-construction papers by Däbritz and Florian Siegl. These cover predicative possession, placeholders, the first-person imperative, verb sequences, the Dolgan–Evenki desiderative, focus position ([Däbritz list](https://www.slm.uni-hamburg.de/ifuu/personen/chris-lasse-daebritz.html)), Nganasan–Evenki/Dolgan parallels ([Siegl 2015](https://digar.ee/arhiiv/en/download/230696)), *barı* 'all', negation ([Siegl 2020](https://tl.harrassowitz-library.com/article/TL/2020/2/7)), *naada* and the partitive ([SUSA](https://journal.fi/susa/article/view/87180)). A 2026 chapter treats the habitual *-AːččI*; its author is unconfirmed ([Benjamins](https://www.benjamins.com/catalog/tsl.136.04sta)). Older work covers description (Ubryatova 1985; Artemyev 2001), the lexicon and word formation (M. Stachowski 1993, 1997), Russian loan phonetics ([K. Stachowski 2010](https://www.ceeol.com/search/article-detail?id=118491)) and Evenki lexical copies ([Hayırsever 2023](https://tl.harrassowitz-library.com/article/TL/2023/2/5)). Gopal, Nichols, Károly & Iosad's acoustic study of rounding harmony, with 96,415 vowels, is in progress ([PDF](https://universiteitleiden.nl/binaries/content/assets/geesteswetenschappen/lucl/vowelharmonyworkshop/gopal-nichols-karoly-iosad.pdf)). **INEL Dolgan 2.0 has 97,625 tokens from 74 speakers**, with tiers for borrowing morphology, code-switching, syntactic function, semantic role and information structure ([documentation](https://s3-uhh.lzs.uni-hamburg.de/gwiss-inel-corpora/remote/dolgan-2.0/documentation/INEL_Dolgan_Corpus.pdf)). **INEL Evenki 2.0 has 93,264 tokens, 34,931 of them Northern (including Taimyr)**, with full glossing and Russian-borrowing annotation on every text, using the same tier scheme ([FDR](https://www.fdr.uni-hamburg.de/record/16605)). This matched design is the main asset for a contact comparison.

### Explicit calls for further research, and their fate

Däbritz 2023 said near-high lax allophones "await acoustic-phonetic studies". Gopal et al. answer this in part (**PARTIAL**). Däbritz 2020 said the contact scenario that produced Dolgan and the intensity of Samoyedic contact are "not fully understood" ([FULL](https://full.btk.ppke.hu/index.php/FULL/article/download/91/99)) (**OPEN**). Siegl 2015 called his contact explanations "speculative" and noted that Taimyr Evenki was undocumented. The documentation point is **PARTIAL**, because INEL Evenki now includes Taimyr. The contact-duration point is OPEN. Siegl's own claim of a 20th-century shift from dative to instrumental in secondary predication, under Russian influence, has **never been tested quantitatively**: a citing-works search found no follow-up. Siegl's remark that Siberian partitives were overlooked is PARTIAL, filled for Dolgan by his own paper and extended contrastively in Sheifer & Bolshakova's 2023 slides ([slides](https://blogs.helsinki.fi/language-diversification-and-spread-north/files/2023/09/Sheifer-DOM-in-DLG.pdf)). **Stapert 2013's own desiderata are UNVERIFIED**: chapters 2–10 are listed on the Leiden repository without titles and could not be opened ([Leiden item](https://scholarlypublications.universiteitleiden.nl/access/item%3A2940584/view)).

### Dolgan against Sakha

Sakha has formal-theoretical work on case: dependent case plus agreement ([Baker & Vinokurova 2010](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11049-010-9105-1)), the partitive as structural case ([Zenodo](https://openpolar.no/Record/ftdatacite:10.5281%2Fzenodo.4680300)), lexical categories and argument structure ([Vinokurova 2005](https://research-portal.uu.nl/en/publications/lexical-categories-and-argument-structure-a-study-with-reference-/)), and indirect evidentiality ([Efremov 2010](https://www.philology.nsc.ru/journals/spj_en/article.php?id=390)). Its contact work concerns Sakha's influence on Even ([Pakendorf 2009](https://benjamins.com/online/ebop/publications/52447)). Dolgan has descriptive grammar coverage of the same categories but no formal-theoretical treatment and no quantitative one. **The divergence is about method and theory, not description.** Däbritz 2023 already describes three-way DOM (nominative/accusative/partitive) and an evidential system "relatively simple compared to surrounding Siberian languages", but reports no frequencies or cross-speaker variation. The Sakha column was not mapped systematically (converbs, morpheme order, passives), so other divergences may exist.

## The maps diverge most where theory, not description, is missing

The four comparisons diverge in different ways. For **Texistepec**, the gap is the largest in absolute terms: a 719-page Sierra Popoluca grammar against a sketch, a dictionary and two theory papers. Even so, the one formal topic Texistepec does have (Reilly's split) has more theoretical attention than its documentation would predict. For **Kamas**, the apparent gap with Tundra Nenets in object conjugation and the dual is **partly an artefact**: Kamas largely lacks the category. The real divergence is that Kamas research sits in two separate layers, with pre-shift grammar written by Klumpp and post-shift corpus work by INEL, and few studies span both. For **Selkup**, the divergence from Nenets is the smallest. Hamburg has already brought corpus statistics to possession, definiteness, DOM, converbs and the optative, so the remaining distance lies in verbal derivation and contact morphology. For **Dolgan**, description is now at Sakha's level, but the formal-theoretical layer (dependent case, argument structure) and the quantitative layer are both missing.

Across all four, the comparison literature contains three kinds of work. The first is multifactorial or theory-driven studies of a single category, like Dalrymple & Nikolaeva on Nenets objects or Baker & Vinokurova on Sakha case. The second is contact studies with a defined donor and recipient, like Pakendorf on Sakha–Even. The third is reference grammars that describe derivation in its own chapters. The target languages have the corpora to support the first two kinds but have mostly received descriptive single-construction papers. That is why the verified angles cluster around *quantifying* claims already made qualitatively: Bolsunovskaya on Selkup Aktionsart, Siegl on Dolgan case, Reilly on Texistepec agreement, Klumpp on Kamas valency. The same clustering is the main risk. Each quantitative paper must cite and engage the qualitative predecessor whose claim it tests.

## Limitations: what "OPEN-VERIFIED" does and does not mean

Every verdict in this report rests on web searches and fetches run through a restricted proxy. Several key sources could not be reached. Glottolog bibliographies for kama1351, selk1253, texi1237 and dolg1241 were not on the allowlist or rendered empty without JavaScript. ISTINA records for Kamas, Selkup and Dolgan needed JavaScript. Stapert 2013's chapters were blocked (403, and the titles were not exposed). Zavala & Wichmann's 2025 chapter, Reilly 2007 on ScienceDirect, the BLS 30 PDF, the DUMAS mémoire, the SAGE/Forker appendix, Klumpp's Tartu ETIS profile and the OUP Kamas chapter were all unreachable. **Google Scholar, Semantic Scholar and OpenAlex cited-by checks were impossible for every language**, so forward-citation checks were approximated by title searches. Budzisch 2021 rendered only through chapter 4, so the section that downgraded Selkup angle S3 was identified by its table of contents, not read. Hungarian-language literature was barely searched (one Selkup query, a few for Kamas). The Tundra Nenets and Sakha comparison columns are incomplete: Salminen's Nenets inflection work and the Sakha literature on converbs, passives and morpheme order were not mapped. Therefore **"OPEN-VERIFIED" means only that no falsifier was found in the searches that were run**. These were typically five to fifteen queries per angle in two to four languages. It is not proof that no such work exists. Conference talks, work-in-progress lists and cross-linguistic sample tables produced most of the downgrades, and these are exactly the sources least visible to search. Some counts (Texistepec DoReCo tokens, Kamas DoReCo tiers, recoverability of INEL birth years) must be confirmed by downloading the data. Finally, the contacts named here are simply the researchers whose work most overlaps each angle. Writing to them before submission is the most reliable remaining check on novelty.

## Conclusion

Verification changed the question these two linguists should ask. It is no longer "what has nobody studied?" but "what has somebody described without counting?" Every surviving angle turns a qualitative claim into a test: Bolsunovskaya's northern allomorphy, Siegl's dative-to-instrumental shift, Reilly's phonological conditioning, Däbritz's simple evidential system. The open corpora (INEL, DoReCo) make such tests cheap. What protects novelty is a disciplined prior-art sweep that includes talks, work-in-progress pages and the language lists of cross-linguistic papers, not a bigger model.

The four languages also need different strategies. Dolgan and Selkup are ready for theory-driven, statistically modelled papers now. Texistepec rewards a single sharp test of a published claim but cannot support claims about variation. Kamas is best used as a contrast case of attrition, where the computational contribution is careful quantification of a 46,500-word idiolect against a qualitative pre-shift baseline, not a balanced statistical comparison. A pair that runs D1 and S1 in parallel and spends an afternoon on the ISTINA and Stapert checks for D3 would be working on the most defensible part of this frontier.
