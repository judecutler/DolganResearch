## Verdict for web Claude

No passage counts **case marking** (case choice on objects, instrumental, dative, partitive, secondary predicates) by speaker age, generation or recording period. The closest passage is #1: Stapert checked speaker age for a case-related nominal phenomenon (stem allomorphy in case-inflected relational nouns) and reports, without figures, that age plays no role. The only counts by age (#2) are for OV/VO word order, which involves objects but not their case. Whether passage #1 turns the Study B novelty claim to PARTIAL is web Claude's call. My reading is that it does not count case by age, but the claim should cite pp. 187-188 and Table 7.3.
