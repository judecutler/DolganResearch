Coding guide (INEL / GRAID conventions)
SSubject of a main-clause predicate (verbal, nominal, adjectival, pronominal or particle predicate). Tag the head of the phrase only (usually its last word).
ODirect object of a main-clause predicate, whatever its case: accusative, partitive or unmarked. Tag the head only.
NAnything else: modifiers inside a phrase, possessors, adverbials, predicates, and all words inside subordinate clauses (converb, participle, relative, complement, temporal, conditional, purpose clauses). INEL tags arguments only in main clauses.
UYou cannot decide. Add a note. Unsure items are discussed together.

Decide from the meaning (translation and glosses), not from the case ending: an unmarked noun can be an object. Orange words are the ones the model is unsure about; the grey label under each word is its guess. You only need to decide the orange words, but you can correct any word. Speaker, date and text are hidden on purpose: do not look them up.
